About

I'm a Singapore-based LNG Trading Analyst with deep front-office experience spanning physical LNG, natural gas, oil, and energy derivatives across ENGIE, Woodside Energy, and Centrica LNG.

Armed with an NUS Master of Financial Engineering, I apply rigorous quant methods — stochastic calculus, option pricing, machine learning — to real commercial problems: pricing exotic structures, evaluating contract optionality, and finding alpha in the LNG basis.

I'm obsessive about automating what shouldn't be manual. I build Python-based pricing tools, curve construction frameworks, and optimization dashboards used directly by front-office traders.

On the side, I prototype systematic trading signals on energy/equity ETFs, and explore how LLMs can be applied to market intelligence and decision-support workflows.
